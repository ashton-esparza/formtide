# FormTide

**Progress comes in waves.**

FormTide is a modern fitness coaching platform designed to make personalized training, nutrition guidance, and long-term progress more accessible, sustainable, and effective.

Fitness is not a linear journey. Whether building muscle, losing body fat, maintaining results, or simply developing healthier habits, every phase serves a purpose.

FormTide helps clients navigate those phases through structured coaching, intelligent automation, and measurable progress.

## Vision

Build an accessible, scalable fitness coaching ecosystem that combines evidence-informed training principles, personalized guidance, and technology.

The goal is to empower everyday individuals to take control of their fitness journey while reducing the administrative overhead traditionally associated with personalized coaching.

**The philosophy is simple: progress comes in waves, and every phase is part of the journey.**

## Core Principles

- **Personalization:** Training and nutrition recommendations should reflect individual goals, capabilities, and circumstances.
- **Sustainability:** Focus on long-term progress instead of short-lived transformations.
- **Education:** Help clients understand the reasoning behind recommendations.
- **Adaptability:** Programs should evolve as goals, performance, and circumstances change.
- **Accessibility:** Make effective coaching approachable for individuals of all experience levels.
- **Scalability:** Leverage automation and intelligent workflows to improve the coaching experience without sacrificing quality.

## Planned Capabilities

- Personalized training programs
- Nutrition guidance and goal tracking

- Body composition and measurement tracking
- Asynchronous check-ins and coach feedback

### Coaching & Administration

- Client management and progress dashboards
- Program creation and management
- Structured check-in review workflows
- Client communication and feedback

### Intelligence & Automation

- Adaptive training and nutrition recommendations
- Progress trend analysis

- Coach-reviewed recommendations where appropriate

These represent potential capabilities rather than a committed implementation roadmap.

## Product Philosophy

FormTide recognizes that progress takes different forms during different phases of a fitness journey.

**Build:** Develop strength, muscle, and physical capacity.

**Lean:** Reduce body fat while prioritizing muscle retention and sustainable habits.

**Maintain:** Consolidate progress, support recovery, and establish a sustainable baseline.

These phases are illustrative, not rigid requirements. Each client's journey is individual, and goals may extend beyond body composition.

The initial focus is establishing a reliable foundation for client onboarding, personalized coaching, progress tracking, and asynchronous feedback.
